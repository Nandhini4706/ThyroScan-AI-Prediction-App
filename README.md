Three important thyroid blood tests

TSH – Thyroid Stimulating Hormone

Helps identify whether the thyroid may be underactive or overactive. Usually the initial test.

T3 – Triiodothyronine

One of the thyroid hormones. It is useful particularly when hyperthyroidism is suspected.

T4 – Thyroxine

The main hormone produced by the thyroid gland. Free T4 is commonly used to assess thyroid function.

A simplified interpretation:
Blood-test pattern	Possible meaning
High TSH + low Free T4	Primary hypothyroidism
Low TSH + high Free T4 and/or T3	Hyperthyroidism
Abnormal TSH + normal Free T4	Possible subclinical thyroid dysfunction; requires clinical interpretation

TSH and T3/T4 must be interpreted together, considering the patient's situation.
